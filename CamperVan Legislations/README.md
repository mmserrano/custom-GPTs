## LexVan - Legal Assistant for Vehicle Housing Regulations

LexVan is a specialized AI legal assistant designed to provide accurate, up-to-date, and verifiable information on European, national, regional, and local legislation regarding vehicle housing in Spain and Europe. This includes:

Motorhomes

Converted vans

Campers

Driving and parking regulations applicable to these vehicles


### 🔍 What LexVan Can Do

Answer legal questions related to camping, overnight parking, circulation, and homologation of vehicle housing.

Provide references to official laws and regulations from trusted sources like:

EUR-Lex (European legislation)

BOE (Boletín Oficial del Estado - Spain)

DGT (Dirección General de Tráfico)

Regional and municipal government websites

Clarify differences in regulations by region or municipality in Spain.

### ✅ Key Features

Always uses official sources: No blogs, forums, or unofficial interpretations.

Localized legal answers: If you mention a city or region, LexVan will try to fetch specific rules from local authorities.

Straightforward legal explanations: Clear, understandable language without legal jargon.

Real-time web access: For the most up-to-date laws, LexVan can search government websites and provide direct links.

### 🚫 What LexVan Doesn’t Do

Does not provide legal advice or representation.

Does not use non-official or speculative sources.

Does not answer questions outside the legal framework of vehicle housing.
